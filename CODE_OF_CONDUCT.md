# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository, issue, pull request and discussion in the Tunedness
organization, and to any other space where someone is representing the project.

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about the code, not about the person writing it. Assume the
other side is acting in good faith until it is clear they are not.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why it matters, what would be better.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and that a contributor may say no
  to a request for more work.
- Respect that people here write in a second language, work in other time zones, and
  are usually doing this alongside a job.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private, including unwanted contact after being
  asked to stop.
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on age, disability, ethnicity, gender
  identity or expression, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual
  orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately derailing a discussion, or repeatedly reopening a settled decision to
  wear people down.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the organization's maintainer through
[muhammetsafak.com.tr](https://www.muhammetsafak.com.tr/en/). Reports are read by the
maintainer only, and the reporter's identity is not shared with the person reported.

Describe what happened, where, and roughly when. Links to the relevant comments help.
You do not need to have a conclusion ready — reporting an uncomfortable interaction is
enough to start a conversation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what to do, in rough order of severity: a private word, a public
correction, an edit or removal of the content, a temporary block, a permanent ban from
the organization. Where the intent was clearly malicious or the harm serious, the first
step can be the last one.

Enforcement decisions are not up for public debate; if you think one was wrong, say so
through the same private channel.
